## English (en-US)

**Name:** Ink Notes

**Short description:**
Simple notes: checklists, tables and formatting, synced to your Drive.

**Full description:**

Ink Notes is a simple, fast and private notes app. Write, organize and find your ideas without distractions.

WRITE YOUR WAY
• Titles, headings, bold, italic, underline and strikethrough
• Bulleted lists, numbered lists and checklists
• Tables to organize information
• Checked items move to the bottom; long-press to reorder
• Undo and redo
• Tappable links, email addresses and phone numbers

STAY ORGANIZED
• Folders to keep notes apart
• Pin important notes to the top
• Swipe to pin or delete
• List or gallery view
• Search across all your notes
• Recently Deleted: recover a note for 30 days

CAPTURE QUICKLY
• Home screen widget
• Quick Settings tile to start a note
• Shortcuts for a new note or a new checklist
• Share text from any app to save it as a note

YOUR NOTES ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
Turn on Google Drive sync and your notes are stored in a private folder in your own Drive. Ink Notes has no servers: nobody but you can see your notes.

MADE FOR ANDROID
• Material You: colors follow your wallpaper
• Light, dark and pure black themes for OLED screens
• Works with TalkBack

No ads. No tracking. No account required.
